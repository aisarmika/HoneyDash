How It Works

1 · The Trap

InterNetworks HoneyDash exposes decoy services — a fake SSH server, web server and database — that look real to attackers but hold no real data. Every connection to them is an intruder.

2 · The Capture

When attackers probe, log in, or run commands, every move is recorded: the country they came from, the protocol they targeted, and the credentials they tried.

3 · The Intelligence

Each attack is enriched with geolocation, scored for severity, and screened by a machine-learning model that flags unusual behaviour — turning raw noise into clear insight.
